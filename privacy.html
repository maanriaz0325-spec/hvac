Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Miami Air Conditioning Repair ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share through our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website, submit a contact form, request an estimate, or contact us about HVAC services, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone number, email address, and service address.
  • Service Details: Information about your air conditioning, heating, ventilation, or other HVAC needs.
  • Communication Records: Messages, inquiries, and other information you provide when communicating with us.
  • Technical Information: Your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and general website usage data.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through general website forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected to:

  • Respond to service inquiries and questions.
  • Schedule appointments and coordinate service requests.
  • Prepare estimates and provide information about requested HVAC services.
  • Communicate about appointments, services, and follow-up inquiries.
  • Improve website functionality and user experience.
  • Monitor website performance and help protect against spam, fraud, or unauthorized activity.
  • Meet applicable legal and business obligations.

We do not use information for purposes unrelated to this policy without an appropriate legal basis or your consent where required.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand website traffic, and improve site performance.

We may also use analytics or advertising services that collect information about how visitors interact with our website.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

4. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share information when reasonably necessary with:

  • Service Providers: Companies that help operate our website, manage communications, provide analytics, or support business operations.
  • Service Partners: Contractors or technicians involved in responding to or fulfilling a service request, where applicable.
  • Legal Authorities: Government agencies or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and safety.
  • Business Transfers: A successor or acquiring organization if our business undergoes a merger, sale, or similar transaction.

We expect service providers handling information on our behalf to use it only for the services they provide, subject to applicable requirements.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no website, online service, or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, manage service relationships, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

7. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we maintain about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information.
  • Opt out of certain types of data processing where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request information about how your personal information is collected and shared.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us using the details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

8. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services. These websites and services have their own privacy policies and practices.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our website and HVAC services are intended for a general audience and are not directed toward children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our website. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

10. Notice to Florida Residents

Residents of Florida and other states may have additional privacy rights under applicable state laws. Depending on the law and whether it applies to our business, these rights may include access, correction, deletion, or opting out of certain data processing activities.

You may contact us to submit a privacy request or ask questions about how your information is handled.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
