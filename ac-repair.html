DIAGNOSTIC GUIDANCE

AC Repair – Common Air Conditioning Problems

Air conditioning problems can affect your comfort in different ways. You may notice warm air coming through the vents, water collecting around the indoor unit, unusual equipment noises, or a system that keeps switching on and off.

We help customers with common AC problems, including:

  • Air conditioner not turning on
  • AC running but not cooling
  • Warm air coming from the vents
  • Water leaking from the indoor unit
  • Weak airflow or uneven cooling
  • Buzzing, rattling, or grinding noises
  • Outdoor fan or indoor blower not working
  • Frozen evaporator coils or ice buildup
  • Frequent AC cycling (turning on and off)
  • Air conditioner running continuously

1. AC Not Turning On or Starting

Common Problems:
No Startup Response: Your thermostat is set correctly, but the air conditioner does not turn on.
Outdoor Unit Not Running: The indoor air handler operates while the outdoor condenser remains off.
Interrupted Startup: The system clicks, attempts to start, or shuts down before beginning its cooling cycle.
How We Help:
Check controls and power supply: We assess thermostat settings, electrical supply, safety switches, and relevant controls.
Inspect affected components: We check capacitors, contactors, wiring, and fan motors.
Explain repair options: We explain recommended work and provide an estimate before proceeding.

2. AC Running but Not Cooling

Common Problems:
Warm Air From Vents: Your air conditioner runs but blows warm or insufficiently cool air.
Poor Cooling Performance: Your home or business struggles to reach the thermostat setting.
Extended Cooling Cycles: The system runs continuously without maintaining the desired indoor temperature.
How We Help:
Assess cooling performance: We examine system operation, airflow, thermostat response, and relevant components.
Investigate refrigerant problems: If testing indicates a refrigerant issue, we check for leaks or incorrect charge rather than just topping off.
Recommend appropriate repair: Solutions may involve correcting airflow, repairing a refrigerant leak, or fixing a faulty component.

3. AC Leaking Water or Having Drainage Problems

Common Problems:
Water Around Indoor Unit: You notice dripping or pooling around the air handler.
Overflowing Drain Pan: Water collects in the drain pan instead of draining properly.
Condensate Drain Backup: A blocked drain line or faulty drainage component prevents moisture from leaving the system.
How We Help:
Inspect drainage system: We check condensate drain lines, drain pans, and relevant components to identify blockages or back-ups.
Check underlying causes: We assess the float switch, condensate pump, and possible ice buildup contributing to the overflow.
Recommend necessary correction: Clearing line blockages, repairing cracked pans, or addressing drainage slope issues.

4. Weak Airflow or Uneven Cooling

Common Problems:
Weak Air From Vents: Certain rooms receive noticeably less conditioned air.
Uneven Room Temperatures: Some areas remain warm while others cool properly.
Restricted Air Distribution: Blocked filters, return-air problems, blower faults, or ductwork issues interfere with airflow.
How We Help:
Check airflow restrictions: We assess air filters, return-air paths, blower operation, and accessible system parts.
Investigate uneven room temperatures: We evaluate possible duct leakage, airflow imbalance, or distribution issues.
Identify suitable repairs: Correcting airflow restrictions, fixing blower or coil issues, or repairing damaged ducts.

5. AC Making Strange Noises or Electrical Problems

Common Problems:
Buzzing or Humming: Unusual electrical sounds may indicate a problem with starting or operating components.
Rattling or Grinding: Loose parts, fan blades, or motor problems can create mechanical noise.
Starting or Power Issues: Repeated breaker trips or a fan that will not spin may indicate an electrical fault.
How We Help:
Identify source of noise: We assess indoor and outdoor equipment to locate whether noise comes from fans, motors, or mounts.
Inspect electrical components: We test capacitors, contactors, wiring, relays, and motor connections with multimeters.
Explain repair needed: We identify confirmed faults and discuss available repair options before doing approved work.

6. AC Keeps Turning On and Off, Freezing Up, or Running Continuously

Common Problems:
Frequent On-and-Off Cycling: The air conditioner starts and stops repeatedly instead of completing normal cooling cycles.
Ice on Coils or Refrigerant Lines: Frost or ice buildup can restrict system operation and reduce cooling.
Continuous Running: The AC operates for extended periods without maintaining the selected temperature.
How We Help:
Assess system operation: We check thermostat response, system settings, and cycling behavior under load.
Investigate ice buildup: If the unit is freezing, we inspect for airflow blockages and refrigerant pressure imbalances.
Address underlying issue: We explain findings and recommend the appropriate repair or comprehensive assessment.
