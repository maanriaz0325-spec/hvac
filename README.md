# Cool Comfort Heating & Air Conditioning — Website

A responsive, high-converting HVAC contractor website crafted according to the design specification and reference image.

## Updated Features & Architecture

- **Strict Inter Typography & Letter-Spacing**:
  - **Headings**: Inter (Bold / Weight 700) with tight kerning (`letter-spacing: -0.028em` to `-0.035em`) for a confident, established contractor look matching the typography reference.
  - **Body Copy**: Inter (Regular / Weight 400) with generous, open line-height (`line-height: 1.65`) for effortless scanning.
- **Hero Section**:
  - Authentic residential HVAC service niche photography: Licensed technician in uniform servicing outdoor AC condenser with diagnostic gauge manifold.
  - Floating 24/7 Emergency Service badge.
- **Complete Heating & Cooling Solutions**:
  - **4 compact boxes per row** (2 rows of 4 = 8 services total) with sleek proportions and dedicated HVAC imagery.
  - Services: AC Repair, AC Installation & Replacement, Heating & Furnace Repair, Heat Pump Services, Annual Maintenance & Tune-Up, Air Duct Cleaning, Indoor Air Quality & Filtration, and Commercial HVAC Services.
- **"We Repair & Install Every Major AC Brand" Section**:
  - Placed directly **above** the Why Choose Us section.
  - Showcases 8 top brands: Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, York, Daikin, and American Standard.
- **5 Real Accreditation & Trust Badges**:
  - Placed directly **below** the Why Choose Us section.
  - Badges: BBB Accredited (A+ Rating), NATE Certified, EPA Universal Section 608, Energy Star Partner, and Google Guaranteed.
- **Residential & Commercial Split Property Cards**:
  - Side-by-side horizontal cards with home & commercial building imagery, bold titles, concise descriptions, and bright red CTA buttons (*"Residential Services →"* and *"Commercial Services →"*).
- **"What Our Customers Say" (Reviews Slider)**:
  - 6 verified customer reviews organized into 2 slides (3 per slide on desktop).
  - Users can swipe on mobile or click the Prev/Next navigation buttons and pagination dots.
- **Split FAQ Layout**:
  - Left column: Headings, explanatory text, and quick support dispatch card.
  - Right column: Clean vertical accordion stack with smooth expand/collapse.
